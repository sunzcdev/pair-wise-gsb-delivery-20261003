Added
-----

*   Add a ``dedup`` view to the parse result, deduplicating entries that
    share an explicit ``id``/``guid``. Entries without an identity are
    kept as-is, the newest entry wins when update times are comparable,
    and unresolvable content conflicts are recorded with the indices of
    the original entries. The original ``entries`` list is never
    modified. Also available as ``feedparser.deduplicate_entries()``.
